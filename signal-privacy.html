Signal+

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Overview

Signal+ is a protein timing reminder built to help you maintain muscle through consistent mTOR activation. Your privacy is straightforward: we do not share or sell your personal data.

Information We Collect

Signal+ collects only what is needed to run your account and reminders:

  • Email address — for sign-in and account identification
  • Profile details you provide — preferred name, sex, and date of birth
  • Protein logs and timestamps — synced so reminders and history work across devices
  • Push notification subscription data — if you enable reminders
  • Subscription and billing status — handled through Stripe when you subscribe
  • Referral code — if someone referred you to Signal+

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising networks or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Data Stored in Your Account

Account data is stored securely in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render. Some app state — such as recent log timing — may also be stored locally on your device so the app stays fast and works offline.

Data is tied to your account and is used only to operate Signal+. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@signalplushealth.com.

Notifications

Signal+ uses your browser or device's native push notification system to send mTOR timer and hydration reminders. Notification payloads do not include personal health details.

Reminders can be turned on or off in Settings inside the app.

Third Parties

Signal+ uses a small set of service providers to operate the app:

  • Render — app hosting and database
  • Resend — transactional email for secure sign-in links
  • Stripe — subscription billing and payment processing

These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service. We do not include advertising networks, analytics trackers, or other third-party SDKs in the app.

Children

Signal+ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at support@signalplushealth.com
